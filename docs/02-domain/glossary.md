# Glossary

## Official Match
A competitive football match recognized as official by the relevant competition or governing body.

## Friendly Match
A non-competitive match, including club friendlies, international friendlies, preseason matches, and exhibitions. Excluded from MVP statistics.

## Competition
An official tournament or league in which teams participate.

## Season
A defined competition period used to group matches and standings.

## Match
An official fixture between two teams containing competition, season, date, teams, result, goals, cards, and additional match context.

## Regular Time
The initial 90 minutes including normal stoppage time. The MVP statistical result is based on the score at this point.

## Extra Time
Additional playing time used in certain knockout matches. If the match is level after regular time, the MVP statistical result remains a draw.

## Penalty Shootout
Penalty kicks used to determine a winner after extra time. Shootout goals are not match goals and are stored separately.

## Statistical Result
The MVP result based on the score at the end of regular time: win, draw, or loss.

## Final Result
The actual final outcome, including extra time or a penalty shootout.

## Head-to-Head (H2H)
The historical record of official matches between two specific teams.

## Recent Form
Performance across a team's most recent N eligible official matches. The default use case is 10 matches.

## Analysis Scope
The rules defining which matches enter a statistical calculation, including sample size, competition, home/away, and H2H.

## All Competitions
All eligible official competitions in the selected dataset. Non-official fixtures are excluded.

## Home Match
A match where the selected team is the home team.

## Away Match
A match where the selected team is the away team.

## Market
A betting category with a set of possible selections, such as 1X2 or Over/Under.

## Selection
A specific outcome within a market, such as Home, Draw, Away, 1X, X2, Over, or Under.

## 1X2
A market with Home, Draw, and Away selections.

## Double Chance
A market combining two 1X2 outcomes: 1X, X2, or 12.

## Over/Under
A market comparing a match statistic with a defined line, such as goals or cards.

## Most Cards
A market concerning which team receives more cards: Home, Draw, or Away.

## Total Cards
The combined number of cards received by both teams. Yellow and red cards are tracked separately.

## Yellow Card
A disciplinary card issued during a match.

## Red Card
A disciplinary dismissal issued during a match.

## Opening Odds
The earliest recorded bookmaker odds available for a market selection. This is the primary odds target for the MVP.

## Closing Odds
The final recorded bookmaker odds before a market closes. Outside the initial MVP.

## Bookmaker
The betting operator providing a market and odds.

## Implied Probability
A probability derived from decimal odds. It represents the probability implied by the quoted price before accounting for bookmaker margin.

## Raw Data
Recorded facts obtained from data sources, such as results, goals, cards, and odds.

## Statistic
A value calculated from raw data, such as win rate, average goals, or Over frequency.

## Insight
An interpretation derived from one or more statistical measurements and supported by identifiable evidence.

## Decision Support
Analysis intended to help a user evaluate a decision without automatically making it.

## Statistical Engine
The component responsible for calculating statistics from validated historical match data.

## Decision Engine
The component responsible for combining statistical evidence and market information to generate decision-support insights. AI predictions are not part of the initial MVP.

## Sample Size
The number of eligible matches included in a calculation, such as N=5, N=10, or N=20.

## Clean Sheet
A match in which a team concedes zero goals during the statistical match period.

## Statistical Frequency
The proportion of observations in which a defined event occurred. Observed frequency should not automatically be interpreted as true probability.

## Probability Model
A future component that may estimate probabilities using statistical or probabilistic methods. It is distinct from the initial descriptive statistics engine.

## AI Prediction
A prediction generated using artificial intelligence or machine learning. Explicitly outside the MVP scope.
